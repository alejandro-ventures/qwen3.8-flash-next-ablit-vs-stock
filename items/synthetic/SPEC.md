# Illustrative synthetic stand-ins

These new items have not been run on either arm. They do not reproduce or explain the original own-workload scores, which remain non-reproducible descriptive evidence. Never join their new IDs to the original score IDs.

`generate.py` uses Python's `random.Random` with published seed 20261110. Generate 70 JSON-copy items: 50 non-thinking and 20 thinking, with a fixed generic SYNREC record prefix, 48-bit hexadecimal record numbers and two-decimal amounts. Generate 59 auxiliary tasks: 19 two-sentence summaries, 20 five-way support-message classifications, and 20 invoice-field extraction tasks. Templates and all vocabulary are defined in the generator. Task IDs begin `synjson-` and `synaux-`. People and organisations use only numbered generic roles (Participant and Supplier). A local drafting model reviewed every actual invented name in the first candidate and flagged possible real-surname and small-firm collisions without claiming registry verification. We treated that uncertainty as unresolved and removed all invented person/organisation names and abbreviated prefixes. The final generator contains only numbered role labels and the SYNREC record marker. Its templates are independent of the withheld study items.

Rebuild with `python -B items/synthetic/generate.py`. `MANIFEST-synthetic.json` records SHA-256 for both JSONL files and the seed. Published item text contains no original prompt or reply text. The original 200 Layer 2 prompts are withheld; these auxiliary prompts illustrate the workload shape only and carry no measured Layer 2 result.
